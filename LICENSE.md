## Modifications from Glossa

- `PL.cls`: renamed and adapted for *Philosophical Logic*, with
  anonymous-first submission, standard TeX fonts, and natbib support;
  Glossa-specific production, biblatex, and linguistics features were removed.
- `PL.bst`: renamed and adapted for *Philosophical Logic*, with normalized DOI
  formatting and terminal punctuation.
- `PL-template.tex`: renamed and adapted for the *Philosophical Logic*
  submission workflow while retaining the Glossa template structure.
